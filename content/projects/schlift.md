the weightlifting app i use stopped being free and it made me mad/sad. i spent dozens of hours working on a free alternative to see what it would take to build a native app. it does exactly what i want, and maybe something that you want too.

I got to learn about how native apps work, the google play and apple app store work (hint, they're both annoying), and how to deal with customer feedback while trying to squat 405lb (flex).
