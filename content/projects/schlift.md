the weightlifting app i used stopped being free and it made me mad/sad. instead of paying $19 a year, i spent hundreds of hours working on a free alternative out of spite. it does exactly what i want, and maybe something that others want too. it currently has three users, my entire gym crew.

I got to learn about how native apps work, the google play and apple app store work (hint, they're both annoying), and how to deal with customer feedback while trying to squat 405lb (flex).
