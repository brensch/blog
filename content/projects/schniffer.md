it's impossible to book campsites in yosemite without cybernetic augmentation. everyone in the bay has written this exact same application, mine works too. it runs in discord, has the ability to automatically add to cart, and i let my friends use it to save them subscribing to the paid services doing the same thing that seem pretty clearly immoral.

many camping trips have been a result of this project, it's probably the highest impact piece of code i've written for my life (and yes i wrote most of it before the aipocalypse).
