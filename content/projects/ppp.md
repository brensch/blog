the tesla map ui is designed with the same attitude as everything made by tesla: we know best, do what we say while using our product. i'm sure that holds true sometimes, however they don't know that the passenger on my roadtrip doesn't want to eat taco bell or mcdonalds for dinner while we supercharge.

ppp enables the passenger princess to be protected from the burden of finding an appropriate stop for the culinary desires of the cabin.
